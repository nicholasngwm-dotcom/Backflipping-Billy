The rules

How to Play

Controls

  • WASD to walk, mouse to look around.
  • Hold Space to charge, release to backflip. The power bar goes up and back down, forever.
  • R restarts the level, Esc pauses, Shift frees or recaptures the mouse.

Rules

  • Billy cannot jump. He can only backflip, and he flips away from where he faces.
  • To go forward, face the camera (press S), then flip.
  • Land upright, or Billy lands on his head.
  • Die and you restart the level. Beat a level and it's saved.
  • Beat all levels in a mode to unlock that mode's achievement.
