How to Play

  • WASD to walk, mouse to look around.
  • Billy cannot jump. He can only backflip.
  • Hold Space to charge, release to flip. The power bar goes up and back down, forever.
  • Billy flips away from where he faces. To go forward, face the camera (press S), then flip.
  • Land upright, or Billy lands on his head.
  • Die and you restart the level. Beat a level and it's saved.
  • R restarts the level, Esc pauses, Shift frees/recaptures the mouse.
  • Modes: 👶 Baby (normal) · 😎 Pro Elite (shift lock) · 🗿 Chad (inverted controls) · 🥛 God Level Maxxing (no mouse, camera stuck behind Billy, inverted tank controls).
